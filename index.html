Start a timed rush and get fully inside your selected classroom in 45 seconds. Find the key shown in the HUD in a reachable classroom, then use E to raise the next corridor barrier. The next key appears in an accessible classroom after each barrier rises. Moving carts shuttle across the corridors; contact costs 2 seconds.

Escape, help, and board editing pause the clock. Resume continues the same run; R returns to a fresh ready screen. Explore the square school with no timer, keys, or obstacles. Classroom light switches, desks, chairs, and blackboards remain interactive; look for their outline and prompt within two units.
